PLAN YOUR MONEY

Finances & university payment

Before you apply for a visa or travel, understand the money you may need for tuition, living costs and application-related payments.

WHAT TO DO
  1. Confirm what you owe Warwick and by when.
  2. Review your funding plan: savings, family support, loan or a combination.
  3. Understand how Warwick accepts payments and keep proof of payment.
WHAT YOU MAY NEED
  • Offer/payment instructions
  • Banking or loan information
  • Payment method with sufficient funds
  • Payment confirmation
