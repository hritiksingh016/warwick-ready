FIRST-WEEK ESSENTIALS

SIM & banking

Get connected and set up the financial tools you actually need. Compare options rather than assuming there is one best provider.

WHAT TO DO
  1. Choose a SIM/eSIM based on your expected usage and contract preference.
  2. Check what you need to open a UK bank account.
  3. Keep Indian payment methods active while your UK setup is in progress.
WHAT YOU MAY NEED
  • Unlocked phone
  • Passport/immigration information
  • Proof of address where required
  • Existing bank/card access
