Offer & conditions

Your offer may be conditional. The first job is to understand exactly what Warwick requires you to complete.

WHAT TO DO
  1. Read every condition in your offer letter.
  2. Note the deadline or evidence required for each condition.
  3. Complete the condition and follow Warwick's instructions for submitting evidence.
WHAT YOU MAY NEED
  • Offer letter
  • Required test results or academic evidence
  • Access to your Warwick application/email
OFFICIAL SOURCE
Warwick — International students

Use your offer letter and Warwick instructions as the primary source for your specific conditions.
