LEAVE INDIA

Travel day

The goal on travel day is simple: have the important documents, money and accommodation details accessible without unpacking your entire bag.

WHAT TO DO
  1. Check passport, immigration status and travel documents.
  2. Keep accommodation details and onward transport accessible.
  3. Carry a backup of important documents securely.
WHAT YOU MAY NEED
  • Passport
  • Flight details
  • Accommodation address
  • Payment cards
  • Emergency contact details
