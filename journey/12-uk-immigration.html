ARRIVAL

UK immigration

At the UK border, answer questions clearly and have your travel and study information available if requested.

WHAT TO DO
  1. Follow airport signs for immigration and border control.
  2. Keep your passport and relevant study/accommodation details accessible.
  3. Follow instructions given by Border Force.
WHAT YOU MAY NEED
  • Passport
  • Immigration status/eVisa information
  • University and accommodation details
  • Travel details

GOV.UK — Entering the UK
