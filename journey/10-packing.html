PACK WITH PURPOSE

Packing

Pack for your first weeks, not your entire life. Prioritise documents, essentials, weather-appropriate clothing and things that are expensive or difficult to replace.

WHAT TO DO
  1. Create a cabin-bag document folder.
  2. Separate essentials from items you can buy in the UK.
  3. Check airline baggage rules before final packing.
WHAT YOU MAY NEED
  • Passport and travel documents
  • Offer/CAS/visa information
  • Chargers and adapters
  • Essential medication where appropriate
  • Small first-week kit
OFFICIAL SOURCE
GOV.UK — Entering the UK

Airline restrictions and UK border rules should always be checked against current official guidance.
