CAS

Your Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies is a key part of the Student visa process. Treat it as a milestone, not just another email.

WHAT TO DO
  1. Check what Warwick requires before issuing your CAS.
  2. Monitor university communications for CAS information.
  3. When your CAS arrives, check the details carefully and keep it accessible.
WHAT YOU MAY NEED
  • Unconditional offer where required
  • Passport details
  • Information Warwick requests for CAS
