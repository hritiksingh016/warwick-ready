IMPORTANT

Student visa

This is where timing, documents and costs matter. Use Warwick and GOV.UK as the authority and Warwick Ready as your preparation layer.

WHAT TO DO
  1. Check current eligibility and when you can apply.
  2. Prepare documents and evidence relevant to your circumstances.
  3. Budget for the visa application and Immigration Health Surcharge before you begin.
  4. Submit the application using the official route.
WHAT YOU MAY NEED
  • Passport
  • CAS
  • Financial evidence if required
  • Payment method
  • Programme-specific documents where applicable
OFFICIAL SOURCE
GOV.UK — Student visa

Visa rules and fees change. Check the current GOV.UK page immediately before applying.
