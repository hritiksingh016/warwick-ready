Flights

There is no single best airport for every student. Compare convenience, price, flight availability and the onward journey.

WHAT TO DO
  1. Compare Birmingham and London-area arrival options.
  2. Check student fare and baggage conditions directly with the airline.
  3. Choose an arrival time that makes your onward journey realistic.
WHAT YOU MAY NEED
  • Valid travel documentation
  • Visa/eVisa information as applicable
  • Accommodation address
  • Onward transport plan
