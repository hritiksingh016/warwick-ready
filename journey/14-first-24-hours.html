WELCOME TO WARWICK

First 24 hours

Your first day is about getting safe, connected and comfortable — not completing every task at once.

WHAT TO DO
  1. Check in and locate the essentials in your accommodation.
  2. Find your nearest supermarket and transport stop.
  3. Eat, hydrate and rest before trying to solve everything.
WHAT YOU MAY NEED
  • Accommodation check-in details
  • Phone connectivity
  • Payment method
  • Small set of essentials
