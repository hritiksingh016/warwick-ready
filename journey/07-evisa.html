VISA APPROVED

eVisa

After your immigration status is granted, follow current UKVI instructions to access and check your eVisa.

WHAT TO DO
  1. Follow UKVI instructions to create or access your account.
  2. Link your immigration status using the current process.
  3. Check that your personal details and status are correct.
  4. Keep your digital status information accessible for travel.
WHAT YOU MAY NEED
  • Passport or travel document
  • Email/phone access
  • Application or immigration details as requested by UKVI
OFFICIAL SOURCE
GOV.UK — View and prove immigration status

UKVI processes change. Use the current GOV.UK instructions when linking or viewing your status.
